Research

Digital intelligence for heterogeneous vehicular networks

I focus on optimization problems in heterogeneous vehicular networks, with an emphasis on digital twins, multi-task federated learning, on-demand matching, and game-theoretic decision making for intelligent transportation systems.

Vehicular Networks Intelligent Transportation Systems Digital Twins Federated Learning Game Theory Resource Allocation

Current Work

On-demand matching for federated learning in HetVNETs

My current work studies on-demand matching mechanisms for federated learning training tasks in heterogeneous vehicular networks, where vehicles, edge infrastructure, and digital twin representations must be coordinated under dynamic network conditions.
